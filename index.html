    FAQ

    Common questions

    What documents do I need for my income-tax return?

    Typically PAN and Aadhaar, bank statements, Form 16 (if salaried), Form 26AS / AIS, and details of investments, loans and other income. We share a checklist suited to your case.

    Do I need GST registration?

    It depends on your turnover, the nature of supplies, and whether you sell inter-state or through e-commerce. Share your business details and we will confirm what applies.

    I have received a notice from the tax department. What should I do?

    Don't ignore it, and note the response deadline. Share the notice and we will explain what it asks for and prepare the reply.

    Can documents be shared online?

    Yes. Documents can be shared on WhatsApp or email, so most work can be handled without visiting the office.

    Do you work with clients outside Ludhiana?

    Yes, including NRIs. Most compliance work is online, so location is rarely a constraint.
